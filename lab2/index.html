Tic-Tac-Toe RL Demonstration

Value-function learning with epsilon-greedy exploration.

RL settings: learning rate α = 0.5, exploration ε = 0.05. The Python implementation trains two agents through self-play.
You are X. Click a square; the browser opponent uses a simple value-guided policy.
Purpose: demonstrate the Tic-Tac-Toe environment, legal actions, rewards, and agent interaction. The training experiment and quantitative results are produced by tic_tac_toe_rl.py.
